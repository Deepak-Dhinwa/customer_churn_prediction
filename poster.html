Telecom Churn

About one in four customers leave. The ones who leave are concentrated in the first year and on month-to-month contracts.

ROC-AUC 0.84FastAPISHAP

Question

  • Who is about to cancel, and which contract terms make that likely?
  • The label is imbalanced, and total charges arrives as text.

Method

  • Tenure bands, a value feature, and a contract-by-payment interaction.
  • SMOTE on the training fold, then a stacking pipeline saved as one file.
  • The API scores new rows with that pipeline, so the encoding does not drift.

Evidence

26%customers who churn
0.84ROC-AUC

What came out of it

  • Month-to-month and the first 12 months carry the risk.
  • SHAP is what turns the score into a reason a retention team can use.
