Telecom Churn

About one customer in four leaves. They cluster in the first year, on month-to-month contracts, paying by electronic check.

ROC-AUC 0.84FastAPISHAP
  1. 01 · CleanTotal charges arrives as text. Tenure is cut into bands a person can name.
  2. 02 · CombineA value feature, and contract crossed with payment method, because those two travel together.
  3. 03 · Balance the fitSMOTE only on the training fold, so the test still looks like real customers.
  4. 04 · Freeze the pipeScaler, encoder, and stack are one file. The API cannot encode a new row a different way.

Question

  • Who is about to cancel, and which contract terms travel with that?
  • The label is uneven: most people stay.

Why this path

  • A model on raw columns would treat “month-to-month” and “electronic check” as separate trivia. Together they are the pattern.
  • Saving the whole pipeline means a later score uses the same codes as training.

What held

26%of customers churn
0.84ROC-AUC of the saved stack
4drivers SHAP keeps naming

Reading the result

  • Short tenure, month-to-month contract, monthly charges, and electronic check are the four reasons the model repeats.
  • 0.84 separates likely leavers from the rest. It does not say which offer would keep them.
  • Logistic regression was about 0.80. A single tree model was about 0.83. The stack adds a small step, and the reason codes are the useful part.

A real output

POST /predict row 1 churn probability 0.82 row 2 stay probability 0.10

Those two rows are the first customers in the public table, scored by the saved pipeline.

What the API loads
