Telecom Churn

About one in four customers leave. The ones who leave are concentrated in the first year and on month-to-month contracts.

ROC-AUC 0.84FastAPISHAP

Question

  • Who is about to cancel, and which contract terms make that likely?
  • The label is imbalanced, and total charges arrives as text.

Method

  • Tenure bands, a value feature, and a contract-by-payment interaction.
  • SMOTE on the training fold, then a stacking pipeline saved as one file.
  • The API scores new rows with that pipeline, so the encoding does not drift.

Evidence

26%customers who churn
0.84ROC-AUC
POST /predictlive score

What the result actually says

  • About 26% of customers churn, roughly one in four. The first 12 months and month-to-month contracts carry most of that.
  • SHAP names four drivers: short tenure, month-to-month contract, monthly charges, and electronic check.
  • The API scores with churn_stack_pipeline.pkl. The scaler and the one-hot encoder are inside that file, so a new row is encoded the same way as training.

What it does not show

  • 0.84 ROC-AUC separates likely churners from the rest. It does not say which offer would keep them.
  • SMOTE balanced the training fold. The live score is still a probability on the original mix of customers.

Beside the other attempts

  • Logistic regression about 0.80. Random forest and XGBoost about 0.83. The stack of those three is about 0.84.
  • The lift from stacking is small. The useful part is the reason code from SHAP, not the third decimal of AUC.

Stack

pandas, scikit-learn stacking, SMOTE, SHAP, FastAPI. A separate LightGBM file from train_model.py is not what the API loads.
